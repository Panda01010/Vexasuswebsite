What happens if I want to cancel?
You can cancel anytime without penalties. The site goes offline. No long-term contracts, no fines, no complications. If you ever want to return, the site will still be saved on my servers.
Will the site appear on Google?
I configure basic SEO for your area and trade. I don't promise the first spot — that takes time and depends on the competition. But you will appear. Today you don't appear at all.

I show you the site before publishing. I fix everything until you're happy with it. I only publish with your explicit approval.

No. You just need to have photos of your work on your phone and tell me what you do. I handle everything else — domain, server, design, text.

Yes. Lisbon, Porto, Setúbal, Algarve, interior — wherever you work, the site works with you.

Yes. I configure your site with the right keywords to capture clients looking for contractors for projects with HPP support or financing. It's a huge market that most contractors aren't exploiting online.

The domain is always yours — it's registered in your name from the start. If you cancel, you only lose the hosting. You can take the site to another server whenever you want.
